# Handelsbanken UK

> Handelsbanken UK (Handelsbanken plc) is the UK relationship bank of Svenska Handelsbanken AB, operating a decentralised network of 200+ local branches. It is authorised by the PRA and regulated by the FCA and PRA (Financial Services Register 806852). Its Great Britain market Open Banking / PSD2 APIs are published to the Berlin Group NextGenPSD2 standard (not the OBIE Read/Write standard used by the CMA9), on the group developer portal at developer.handelsbanken.com. TPPs enrol against the production gateway with a PSD2 eIDAS (QWAC/QSEALC) or UK Open Banking (OBWAC) certificate, using OAuth2 client authentication, mutual-TLS and PSD2 strong customer authentication, and can test against a sandbox first.

## APIs
- [PSD2 Account Information API (AIS)](https://developer.handelsbanken.com/api/psd2): retrieve account details, balances and transactions for consenting GB customers.
- [PSD2 Card Account Information API](https://developer.handelsbanken.com/api/psd2): card account details, balances and transactions.
- [PSD2 Payment Initiation API (PIS)](https://developer.handelsbanken.com/api/psd2): initiate and manage payments from consenting accounts.
- [PSD2 Confirmation of Funds API (CBPII)](https://developer.handelsbanken.com/api/psd2): check whether an amount is available on a consenting account.
- [PSD2 Third-Parties API](https://developer.handelsbanken.com/api/psd2/live): TPP enrolment and certificate attachment for live access.
- [FCA Service Metrics API](https://developer.handelsbanken.com/api/product/1876/api/1816): service-quality metrics for GB business current account services.

## Authentication
- OAuth2 (client-credentials, authorization-code, decoupled grants) over mutual-TLS.
- Production requires a PSD2 eIDAS QWAC or UK Open Banking OBWAC certificate from a QTSP.
- Strong Customer Authentication via Redirect or Decoupled authorization flows.
